# A Little Bit of Everything Team ⛏️

**A Little Bit of Everything Team** *(Lunaside Modding)* is the Minecraft modding initiative of **Not a Glitch Studios**, dedicated to crafting quality mods and modpack expansions for Minecraft Java Edition.

---

## 🏢 Parent Organization

- 🌐 **Main Organization**: [Not a Glitch Studios](https://www.notaglitch.net/) [ᴳᴴ](https://github.com/notaglitchstudios)

---

## 📦 Modpack & Mod Projects

- 🧩 **A Little Bit of Everything** *(WIP Modpack)*  
  The flagship modpack currently under active development.

- ⚙️ **[Andromeda's Create](http://www.andromedacat.net/ac)** *(Modpack)*  
  A Create-focused Minecraft modpack built around automation, contraptions, and exploration. *(Minecraft 1.21.1 — NeoForge)*

- 🎣 **[A Little Bit of Fishing](https://github.com/Lunaside-Modding/a-little-bit-of-fishing)**  
  Fishing companion mod designed for the *A Little Bit of Everything* modpack.

- 🎵 **[A Little Bit of Music](https://github.com/Lunaside-Modding/a-little-bit-of-music)**  
  Standalone music expansion mod bringing extended soundtracks to your world, published by **[Not a Glitch Records](https://music.notaglitch.net/)**.  
  - 🌐 Music Label Website: [music.notaglitch.net](https://music.notaglitch.net/)  
  - 🎧 [Official Release: *"A Normal Hotel"*](https://song.link/albom-a-normal-hotel)  
  - 🎶 [Official Soundtrack Playlist on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLrWRV6g4PHiKpH_m70-92AmyGoufqR7h6)

- 🍎 **[A Little Bit of Delight](https://github.com/Lunaside-Modding/a-little-bit-of-delight)**  
  Farmer's Delight addon introducing additional fruit trees, crops, and culinary options.

- 🎨 **[MDM Dye Depot Compat](https://modrinth.com/mod/mdm-dye-depot-compat)**  
  Compatibility mod bridging MDM and Dye Depot for expanded dyeable content.
